Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 27, 2026 · Last updated: July 27, 2026

Secure Home Base ("the App," "we," "our") is a personal document and household management app. This policy explains what information the App handles and how it's used. We built the App to keep your family's documents and information private and under your control.

Information Stored in the App

The App lets you store information about your household, including:

  • Documents, warranties, and maintenance records you add
  • Family member profiles, including optional free-text notes you choose to add
  • Photos or scans of documents you capture or upload
This information is stored locally on your device. [CONFIRM: The App does not upload your documents, photos, or family member data to our servers or any third party. Data you enter stays on your device unless you choose to export or share it yourself, e.g. via the PDF export feature.]

Document Scanning

The App's scan feature uses on-device text recognition to help auto-fill document fields (such as document numbers and expiration dates). This processing happens entirely on your device — scanned images and recognized text are not sent to us or any external service for this feature.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, the App schedules local notifications on your device to alert you before documents, warranties, or maintenance items expire. [CONFIRM: If using Expo's push notification service] To deliver these notifications, a device push token is registered with Apple's push notification service via our notification infrastructure provider (Expo). This token does not include the content of your documents or personal notes.

Analytics and Crash Reporting

[CONFIRM AND NAME SERVICE, e.g. "We use [Sentry / Expo Application Services] to help us identify and fix crashes and technical issues."] This may include technical information such as device model, operating system version, and app version. This data does not include the contents of your documents, photos, or family member notes.

Data You Control

  • You can delete any document, warranty, maintenance item, or family member profile at any time within the App.
  • Deleting the App from your device removes locally stored data [CONFIRM: unless included in your device's own iCloud/iTunes backup — clarify based on Claude Code findings].
  • We do not sell your data. We do not share your document contents or family information with third parties.

Children's Information

The App may be used to store information about family members, including children, at the discretion of the parent or guardian using the App. This information is stored on the device as described above and is not transmitted to us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date.
